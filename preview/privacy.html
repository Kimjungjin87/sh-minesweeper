Privacy Policy

Mine Marathon · Last updated: October 26, 2026

Mine Marathon is a puzzle game. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game.

What stays on your device

  • Your game progress, settings, gems and picture album are saved in your browser's local storage on your device only.
  • You can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

What is sent to our server (Marathon ranking only)

Only if you choose Submit to ranking after a Marathon run, we store:

  • the name you type (profanity is masked automatically),
  • your result (completion %, time, and how the run ended),
  • a random ID created by the game to keep one entry per player per round.

We do not collect your email, location, contacts or any account information. The ranking is shown publicly to other players. Rankings are stored on Cloudflare and may be removed after each round. To ask for your entry to be removed, contact the developer through the game's page on the platform where you play.

Ads

The game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one. Ads are provided by the game platform (CrazyGames) under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over.
